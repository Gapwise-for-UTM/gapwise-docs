## Checklist

- [ ] Necessity and a no/new-provider alternative assessed.
- [ ] Exact data fields, subjects, sources, purpose, access direction, and excluded data documented.
- [ ] Authentication, authorization, tenant isolation, encryption, key/secret handling, logging, export, deletion, and incident controls evidenced at an appropriate non-sensitive level.
- [ ] Provider subprocessors, processing/storage locations, transfers, and change notification are recorded only from current evidence; unknowns stay HUMAN-CONFIRMATION.
- [ ] Retention, deletion, backup, return/export, and termination behavior reconciled with the schedule.
- [ ] Contract/DPA, confidentiality, security terms, audit evidence, incident cooperation, and legal basis reviewed by authorized humans; no acceptance is performed by this checklist.
- [ ] Provider privacy/security documentation and relevant assurance reports were reviewed for scope, date, exclusions, and independent provenance without turning them into Gapwise certifications.
- [ ] Least-privilege configuration, environment separation, credential rotation/revocation, and offboarding owner defined.
- [ ] User transparency/policy/consent impacts and accessibility considered by humans.
- [ ] Failure, breach, acquisition, discontinuation, and data-return exit paths documented.
