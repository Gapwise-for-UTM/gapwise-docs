# Privacy complaint and request handling template

> **DRAFT — HUMAN/LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED** — Response channels, identity checks, deadlines, remedies, notices, and escalation duties require human/legal approval before use or publication.

## Intake record (restricted)

Record request ID, received timestamp/channel, category, minimal contact reference, affected product/data, immediate safety concern, assigned owner, status, related incident/right-request IDs, and evidence location. Do not copy identity documents or request contents into public issue trackers.

## Handling sequence

1. **Receive safely:** acknowledge only using approved wording/channel; preserve the original in the restricted system.
2. **Triage:** distinguish complaint, access/correction/deletion request, security report, privacy incident, legal demand, and unrelated support. A record may follow more than one workflow.
3. **Verify identity proportionately:** use an approved method; do not collect extra identity data by default. Record method/result, not unnecessary secrets.
4. **Scope:** map the matter to `[PHASE-1: inventory IDs]`, systems, processors, and evidence cutoff.
5. **Contain/escalate:** invoke the incident checklist when exposure or ongoing harm is suspected. Route legal or authority questions to an authorized human.
6. **Investigate:** preserve evidence, restrict access, log queries/actions, and separate verified facts from allegations and hypotheses.
7. **Decide/respond:** human owner approves findings, action, limitations, and wording. Do not promise a deadline or remedy not approved and operationally supported.
8. **Close/review:** record action evidence, communication, unresolved items, lessons, and a retention disposition for the case record.

## Quality gates

- [ ] No retaliation, blame, or unsupported legal conclusion in communications.
- [ ] Accessibility or communication accommodations were considered without assuming needs.
- [ ] Only necessary staff/processors received request data.
- [ ] Response describes known limitations (including backups/providers) accurately.
- [ ] Metrics count only reliably tracked records; unknown is not reported as zero.
