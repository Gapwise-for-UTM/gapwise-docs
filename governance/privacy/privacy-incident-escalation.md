# Privacy-incident escalation checklist

> **DRAFT — HUMAN/LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED** — Notification, reporting, privilege, severity, and timing decisions must be made by authorized humans using current facts and applicable advice. This is not a guaranteed response SLA.

## Immediate actions

- [ ] Create a restricted incident ID; record reporter, discovery time, systems, and evidence locations without placing personal data in public chat/tickets.
- [ ] Assign an incident lead and privacy/security decision makers from the approved contact matrix (`[HUMAN-CONFIRMATION]`).
- [ ] Preserve relevant evidence with access and integrity controls; keep facts, hypotheses, and decisions separate.
- [ ] Contain ongoing exposure using the least destructive reversible action; do not erase evidence or make unreviewed public statements.
- [ ] Consider credential/token revocation, session invalidation, provider escalation, and affected feature isolation with security owners.

## Privacy triage

Record `[PHASE-1: data/system/processor IDs]`; what happened; confirmed versus potentially affected people and records; data sensitivity and protections; unauthorized actor/access/use/disclosure/loss; duration and recoverability; possible harms; ongoing exposure; cross-user/provider/geography uncertainty; and confidence/evidence for each fact. Unknown counts remain unknown, not zero.

## Human decision points

- [ ] Severity and escalation level approved under the current incident plan.
- [ ] Legal/privacy reviewer assessed notification/reporting duties, jurisdictions, recipients, content, and timing.
- [ ] Provider, insurer, institutional, law-enforcement, or other external contact was neither assumed nor initiated without authority.
- [ ] User/support/status communication is accurate, accessible, non-speculative, and approved.
- [ ] Requests/complaints and security response are cross-linked where needed without oversharing.

## Recovery and closure

- [ ] Validate containment and remediation with evidence; monitor for recurrence.
- [ ] Record affected data disposition, processor confirmations, and unresolved backup/log limitations.
- [ ] Conduct an approved post-incident review covering root/contributing causes, control gaps, owners, due dates, and verification.
- [ ] Update Phase 1 inventory, PIA, retention schedule, third-party review, runbooks, and public text when evidence changed.
- [ ] Approve closure and restricted incident-record retention; publish statistics only from a reliable counting process.
