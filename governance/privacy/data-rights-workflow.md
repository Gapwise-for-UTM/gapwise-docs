## Workflow

1. Log and classify the request without collecting more identity data than the approved method needs.
2. Confirm the requester is authorized for the account/data; escalate ambiguity rather than disclosing or deleting.
3. Search the Phase 1 inventory for local-only, account, sync, authentication, diagnostic, AI delegation, processor, backup, and derived records. Clearly identify boundaries Gapwise cannot observe.
4. For **access**, assemble only the approved scope, review it for another person's data, credentials, secrets, or security-sensitive material, and transmit through an approved channel.
5. For **correction**, identify the authoritative source, validate the correction, propagate only where evidenced, and record systems that cannot be changed.
6. For **deletion**, distinguish device-local removal, account deletion, authorization revocation, server records, logs/backups, and processor copies. Follow the approved retention schedule and verify observable outcomes.
7. Require human review for exceptions, refusal, partial fulfillment, extension, legal demand, or conflicting preservation needs.
8. Record completion evidence and approved response; never label an unobservable downstream action complete.
