## If something looks broken

Retry once after a short delay for a transient upstream or network failure. If the problem persists, report user-facing problems to [support@gapwise.ca](mailto:support@gapwise.ca). Suspected vulnerabilities should be reported privately to [security@gapwise.ca](mailto:security@gapwise.ca), not through the public status-data issue.

## Scope

The status surface covers Gapwise-owned application surfaces and direct production dependencies that can be checked safely from outside the private user context. University of Toronto systems and other upstream services remain outside Gapwise's control; when practical, incident notes distinguish upstream degradation from a Gapwise-originated incident.
